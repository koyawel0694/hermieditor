## What has been directly inspected and verified (v3.0 Update)

Direct video downloads and frame-by-frame visual audits were conducted for the primary reference videos:

1. **`kiVGOS7-n_o`: Cupid – Fifty Fifty (Motion Graphic by wanezv - 24s)**
   - *Style Families:* MG-01 (Minimalist Editorial), MG-02 (Kinetic Typography), MG-13 (Visual Metaphors).
   - *Direct Observations:* Words form architectural spaces (e.g. an opening 3D perspective door between words in `"A SIGN"`, casting an angled beam of light along the floor plane). Tactile props with stipple grain shading (rotating blue umbrella for `"lover/rain"`) float with 3D drop shadows. Fluid spring dampening/bounce easing, dynamic curved text paths, and seamless camera fly-throughs. Zero UI cards or boxes.

2. **`FOMWS5pChuA`: Maroon 5 – Maps (Kinetic Typography by Ninh Truong / Red Cat Motion - 32s)**
   - *Style Families:* MG-02 (Kinetic Typography), MG-09 (3D/Isometric Object Motion), MG-14 (Shape/Graphic Transitions).
   - *Direct Observations:* Distinct font personalities per word (`"I'M CHANGING"` in thin geo-sans, `"all of the"` in bubbly bold rounded lower-case, `"STATION"` in italic wide display, `"CONVERSATION"` in bold slab with diagonal retro stripes and extruded 3D drop shadow). Diegetic graphic props house or launch lyrics (animated retro radio for station, speech bubble for conversation, isometric delivery box with 3D word `"ALL"` popping out). Continuous 30°–45° Dutch angle camera flight through dark teal space with sparks and particles.

3. **`51fUZQEifMk`: After The Storm – Kali Uchis ft. Tyler The Creator (3D Motion Graphics by EDrie - 16s)**
   - *Style Families:* MG-07 (Mixed-Media Cut-out Collage), MG-08 (2.5D Parallax), MG-09 (3D Motion).
   - *Direct Observations:* Photographic cut-out collage elements (mossy mountains, sunflowers, clouds, gingham tablecloth, retro cereal box). Words integrated into physical environment (giant yellow slab letters `"AFTER"` anchored behind 3D mountain ridges like the Hollywood sign; credits tethered to and swinging from frayed hemp ropes). Deep multiplane parallax with shallow depth of field blur.

## How a compatible harness should complete the analysis

If it can access video playback or a supplied downloaded/reference file, inspect each video and record:
- Which visual design families appear (minimal callouts, animated diagrams, collage/cut-out, UI motion, tracked annotation, parallax, etc.).
- How often graphic moments appear and how long they remain readable.
- Typography, composition, palette, spacing, line/shape grammar and texture.
- Entrance/build/hold/exit animation; direction, easing, stagger, scale and motion blur.
- Transition types and what motivates each one.
- Sound effects, music accents, rhythm and use of silence.
- Which reference traits fit the current script and which would only be decoration.

If the harness cannot inspect the references, state that limitation. Ask the user for a screenshot, screen recording or local reference file only when shot-level matching is important enough to block the edit. Use the references as design inspiration and reproduce their underlying logic with original assets/compositions rather than copying full scenes or unique assets exactly.
