## Quality gates

- [ ] Every major spoken idea has a useful visual decision.
- [ ] At least half of major explanatory beats use designed or sourced explanatory visuals where the subject supports them (planning target, not a time quota).
- [ ] All values, claims, quotes, and on-screen sources are grounded in real supplied/checked material.
- [ ] Text/labels are readable, correctly spelled, and built from exact text—not AI-rendered text.
- [ ] Graphics animate progressively in narration order and remain on screen long enough to understand.
- [ ] The timeline mixes designed visuals, meaningful footage, and moments of visual rest.
- [ ] Sound effects are selective; voice remains clear.
- [ ] Render dimensions, duration, streams, sync, and final visual/audio quality were checked.
